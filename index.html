Send to Excel

SharePoint

Sends any of this day's rows that aren't in the workbook yet. Rows already sent are left alone.

On the iPhone · Paste into one cell

Copies the whole day as one line. In Excel, open the tab, tap cell Z1, tap the bar at the top where the cell's text shows, tap Select All, then Paste, then the check mark. The formulas fill every row from it. Paste again anytime and the sheet updates to match the app.

On a computer · Copy and paste rows

For Excel on a computer, in a tab without the Z1 formulas. Click the first empty cell in column A and paste.

Send as a file

Makes an Excel file of these rows. In the share menu tap Save to Files and pick your OneDrive or SharePoint folder (or On My iPhone). Then open the file in the Excel app, select rows 2 and down across columns A to X, copy, and paste at A4 of the tab.
